### Price sourcing detail

Primary is `stockanalysis.com/api/symbol/{s|e}/{SYM}/history?range=5Y`. Secondary is
CNBC `quote.cnbc.com` — the market is **closed** on a Saturday, so `last` carries the Friday
close and is gated on `last_time`'s date equalling 2026-07-31; the 2026-07-27 intraday inversion
(where `previous_day_closing` is the right field) does **not** apply to a weekend fire.
Tertiary is Nasdaq `quote-info` `secondaryData.lastSalePrice`.

**IBKR MCP was attempted and failed** — `get_price_snapshot` returned connector server
errors on SPY/QQQ/SOXX and then stopped responding (4 attempts). Per the Price Sourcing
Standard this is a documented failed fetch, not a grounding gap: the standard is satisfied
by two independent web sources agreeing within 1%, and this run has three agreeing within
0.0661%.

### Corporate-action basis (Track B 2026-07-26)

Adjusted close `a` is used for **all** return/indicator math — momentum, RS, realized and
downside vol, beta, tracking error, drawdown, and the `technical_indicators.py` input dir.
Raw close `c` is used for **entry, target and CI prices** and for every settlement. Two CSV
trees are written (`adj/`, `raw/`) because `technical_indicators.py` reads the `Close`
column, so the adjusted values must occupy `Close` in the dir handed to `--history-dir`.

Both trees live in the run scratchpad and are deliberately **not committed** — they are
~72 MB of derived daily bars, and no prior package commits them either. Reproducibility is
preserved without them: `stockanalysis_history_manifest.json` pins the source URL, the
retrieval timestamp, the per-symbol bar count, first/last bar date, last raw and adjusted
close, and the ex-dividend flag for all 519 symbols, and `technical_indicators.json` records
the exact per-ticker source path used at compute time.
