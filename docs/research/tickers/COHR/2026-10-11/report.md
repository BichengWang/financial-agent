## Industry

[INFERENCE] Bandwidth and energy-efficient connectivity are enduring productivity needs, but copper-to-optics penetration is not identical to Coherent's share of the value. Materials/laser devices feed optical modules and systems; accelerator/switch vendors and hyperscalers choose architectures; contract manufacturers and rivals contest the module layer. Copper short links, pluggables, silicon photonics, external lasers, CPO and NPO can coexist and reshape content. Coherent explicitly targets multiple technologies; reliance on one route is not justified [cohr-presentation].

## Decision

**Watchlist, not attractive at the reference price under the retained normalization.** The right-business case is qualified optical infrastructure; Buffett-style durability is plausible but contestable; stewardship shows operating execution while cash spending, legacy acquisition assets and dilution complicate owner returns; Munger-style inversion prioritizes concentration/overbuild; Li Lu's long-term productivity lens supports photonics relevance, not a predetermined equity winner.

Review triggers: OCF converts toward earnings without payables dependence; inventory and receivables stabilize relative to actual shipments; capacity plans generate realized yields and margin; primary individual-customer/proxy records improve coverage; a fully diluted recurring EPS bridge survives disposal-gain removal. Thesis breaks if optical growth continues but required financing and impairment destroy per-share returns. AI research confidence is moderate for stated financials and lower for contract/customer economics; economic certainty remains lower than source abundance. A five-year market closure would require a self-funded durable manufacturing franchise, not merely new design-win headlines.
