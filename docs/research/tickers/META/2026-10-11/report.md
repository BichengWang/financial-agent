## Grounding

NASDAQ META is the publicly traded Class A security; Class B has the same economic participation but ten votes versus Class A's one. [meta-proxy]

## Business

### Who pays and why
**Advertisers pay to reach and convert relevant customers in Meta's large engagement network; they return when measurable outcomes justify their spend, while retaining alternatives in search, retail media, video and other social apps.**
Consumers primarily receive free communication/entertainment services; their attention and activity create the auction inventory and relevance signals.
Business messaging adds payments for customer engagement, while hardware buyers pay for devices and experiences. [meta-fy25] [meta-q2-remarks]

### Economic versus accounting earnings
SBC **$20.427B FY2025**, rising to **$13.690B H1 2026**, boosts OCF through noncash addback but represents compensation/dilution cost. [meta-fy25] [meta-q226]
Capitalized servers/data centers defer costs to depreciation; future utilization and useful lives determine economic earnings, not just current EPS.
Working-capital accruals, deferred taxes, nonmarketable investments and equity-award withholding cash separate OCF from distributable owner earnings.
Advertiser concentration figures, independent incrementality studies and app-level profit/retention cohorts were not observed; these remain primary diligence questions.
Circle of competence covers auction advertising and cash-flow definitions, not the probability that Meta becomes a leading enterprise model provider.

## Management

### Stewardship, control and allocation
Zuckerberg controlled **60.8% voting power** and **99.8% of Class B** at April 1, 2026; economic ownership and voting power are not interchangeable. [meta-proxy]
Founder control supports long-horizon experiments but limits outside owners' ability to stop low-return reinvestment or replace leadership.
The proxy states a voluntarily majority-independent board/independent compensation committee, but those structures do not remove the controlling vote.
CEO $1 salary and no new bonus/equity grant align through existing ownership; security and related-aircraft benefits remain real costs, not proof of either abuse or virtue. [meta-proxy]
Other executives' four-year service-vesting RSUs support retention, but are not an explicit hurdle on incremental capital returns; compensation was high relative to peers. [meta-proxy]
Private-aircraft arrangements/related-party approval policies are disclosed; independent reasonableness of cost and board effectiveness is not verified here.

## Inversion

### Strongest opposing thesis
A knowledgeable bear need not deny good advertising: **AI can improve sales while consuming most cash, entrenching capital intensity before enterprise/agent revenues are established**.
Q2 strong growth alongside lower operating income and nearly exhausted lease-inclusive FCF directly supports that concern. [meta-q226]
The hardware/frontier-AI analogy to past subsidized platforms is informative but not proof: unlike an unprofitable startup, Meta has a highly profitable existing auction engine.

| Failure path | Mechanism / horizon / severity | Warning, mitigation and model treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Capex treadmill | 2026–2031 depreciation, lease/JV commitments and short server lives overwhelm incremental ad profit; high severity | Demand returns/utilization disclosures, cash conversion; flexible silicon/build timing helps; bear EPS decline and 15x multiple |
| Attention/advertiser displacement | TikTok/video, search, retail media or agents capture time/outcomes over several years | Falling engagement, price/volume divergence, advertiser incrementality; base growth below current ad growth, no terminal monopoly assumption |
| Privacy economics | Reduced signals/consent cause worse relevance and weaker ad pricing; ongoing material exposure | EU uptake and ad-performance comparisons; first-party/aggregate models help but do not nullify consent; bear compression [ec-dma] |
| Legal/youth liability | Damages, compliance costs, product restrictions; timing uncertain and potentially severe | Actual legal charges/trials, regulator decisions; ordinary cost drag modeled, catastrophic product restrictions unmodeled [meta-q226] |
| Control/allocation failure | Founder spends indefinitely on RL/frontier models; multiyear compounding loss | RL losses fail to decline, new adjusted exclusions, continued spend without segment returns; no terminal RL break-even credit [meta-proxy] |
| Antitrust remedy | Appeal may reopen structural/remedial exposure; uncertain discrete tail | FTC filed appeal of November 2025 Meta victory; no later disposition observed; forced separation not assigned invented probability [ftc-appeal] |
| Financing/supply shock | Power/chips/talent constraints or external-capital terms transfer economics away from Meta | Debt, restricted cash, lease obligations, partner guarantees; custom silicon/financing diversification mitigate incompletely [meta-q2-remarks] |

Falsification assumptions, not issuer guidance: two consecutive quarters of falling engagement plus worsening independently measured ad returns would weaken the moat thesis.
A persistent rise in inclusive capex without improving cash conversion/new recurring gross profit would invalidate the base-case allocation thesis.
A failure of RL losses to stabilize/reduce over the coming annual cycles would weaken management's stated discipline; no precise unsupported breakpoint is asserted.
Do not average away a severe integrity event or forced platform shutdown; those can warrant research avoidance irrespective of modeled expected price.
Scenario weights are judgment, not measured failure probabilities; avoid precision theater and anchoring to analyst targets.

## Industry

### Value chain and structural demand
Li Lu's lens favors enduring needs: business discovery, customer acquisition and communication, rather than a claim that every AI product must become indispensable.
Advertisers buy outcomes → Meta aggregates attention/relevance → creators/users supply engagement → chips, cloud, power and talent enable delivery.
Alternative routes include search intent, retail purchase data, short-form entertainment, community platforms and direct commercial messaging.
Alphabet/search, Amazon/retail media and TikTok/video are qualitative competitors here; no unobserved competitor financials or numerical market-share claims are supplied.
Advertisers can redirect budgets quickly; small businesses may value integrated creative/campaign tools, but auction competition limits unconditional price increases.
End-user switching costs differ: moving a family/business messaging network is harder than adding another entertainment app.
Meta's global scale is demonstrated, but high reach also increases exposure to consent rules and lower-monetizing regions. [meta-q226] [meta-q2-remarks]
The Commission's December 2025 undertaking required effective EU choice between fully and less personalized ads, with evidence on implementation still to be evaluated. [ec-dma]
FTC appeal status means the November 2025 win is not assumed to settle long-run antitrust exposure. [ftc-appeal]
No numerical TAM is used: AI assistants, ad tools, business messaging and compute can overlap, so summing promotional markets would double count.
Physical supply constraints and rapidly changing model efficiency make long-range infrastructure ROI more uncertain than user adoption alone. [meta-q2-remarks]
Base-case EPS growth is bounded below current reported ad growth because expense, depreciation, regulation and maturity consume part of the tailwind.
Bull case requires advertiser productivity/WhatsApp monetization to accrue to Meta faster than supplier/talent rents; bear case shows the opposite.
In twenty years Meta could remain a major communications institution, but enterprise API/compute leadership is not necessary or established in this thesis.

## Decision

**Watchlist, not an attractive price case under this conditional model.** Business quality is stronger than the current margin-of-safety evidence.
- **Duan business quality:** clear advertiser utility and repeat spend; moderate-high confidence. Disconfirm with independent declining advertiser incrementality/retention.
- **Buffett durability:** networks/distribution and outcome feedback are real; moderate confidence. Disconfirm with persistent engagement migration and eroding cash economics despite AI improvements.
- **Duan/Buffett stewardship:** strong execution, material founder-control and reinvestment discipline concerns; moderate/low confidence on incremental capital returns.
- **Munger inversion:** strongest issue is cash-consuming growth, not lack of revenue; observe inclusive FCF, lease/JV commitments and depreciation against ad-profit gains.
- **Li Lu opportunity:** communication/customer acquisition endure; enterprise/agents are optional and unverified rather than necessary bull certainty.
- **Price:** modeled base and weighted present values below $718.67; this is a conditional research comparison, not a broker action.

Review triggers: next earnings/revised inclusive capex; independent advertiser studies; EU personalized-ad uptake/results; antitrust appeal developments; disclosed lease/JV guarantees; cash buyback versus SBC trends.
Required diligence: quantify maintenance versus growth capex and asset lives; obtain adjusted-to-GAAP EPS bridge; establish WhatsApp paid-message cohorts/gross margins and agent unit economics.
Also obtain current exact Class A/B share register, more independent quote/cap snapshots, lease liabilities beyond release totals and post-July material legal outcomes.
If trading closed for five years, the ad franchise could support ownership reasoning, but unrestricted capital allocation requires more confidence than this evidence establishes.
No definitive allocation, order, brokerage submission or portfolio execution follows from this report.
