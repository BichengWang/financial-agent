## Business

Consumers/accountants pay for accurate compliant tax preparation; businesses pay for accounting, payroll, payments and financial management; advertisers/lenders pay for Credit Karma distribution. They return because financial history, reconciliation, compliance and accountant familiarity are embedded. Alternatives include other accounting software, professional preparers, free filing and internally assisted AI tools. This mixes subscriptions, annual tax purchases, transactions and financial lead generation—not one uniform recurring SaaS stream. [INTU-annual]

## Inversion

The strongest bear thesis is that price/mix and repurchases disguise weaker consumer tax units while AI or free filing reduces basic-service differentiation and Mailchimp remains a drag.

| Failure mechanism | Timing/severity | Warning | Model treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tax competition/regulatory encroachment | Tax seasons; structural | Continuing unit loss or lower paid conversion despite Live growth | Bear −2% EPS growth / 14x exit |
| SMB weakness and price elasticity | Renewals; material | Higher prices but weaker customer growth/retention | Base 10% per-share growth |
| Credit/referral reversal | Downturn | Credit Karma partner demand weakens or lending losses rise | No assumption all revenue is recurring |
| Mailchimp disappointment | Several years | Standalone guidance fails or impairments emerge | Bull requires real platform execution |
| Accuracy/data failure | Discrete; potentially severe | Filing failures, AI advice errors, lost trust | Unmodeled tail |

Do not annualize spring earnings or count customer-held cash as corporate liquidity. The favorable thesis fails if paid tax demand keeps contracting and QuickBooks cannot sustain customers while compensation/buybacks absorb cash. No unobserved current government filing policy or invented event probability is asserted. [INTU-annual]

## Industry

Value chain: taxpayer/SMB records → bookkeeping, compliance and expert interpretation → filing/payment execution → financial distribution and financing. Reliable regulated execution is the durable demand, not generic AI answers. Rules and accountant ecosystems create switching friction but also liability and pricing constraints. [INTU-annual]

Alternatives span professional preparers, free filing, rival small-business ledgers and enterprise suites. No market-share ranking is asserted. Effective pricing is an observed growth driver, while declining TurboTax units are contrary evidence. Tax seasonality, financial-partner demand and SMB formation matter more than an undifferentiated AI software TAM.

Base assumes accounting/customer durability with modest growth and costs inclusive of compensation. Bull needs AI expert efficiency and mid-market expansion without sacrificing trust. Bear allows regulatory changes, price elasticity and commoditized advice to weaken the wallet. The current status of any government Direct File program is not presented as a sourced fact.

## Decision

Attractive for further diligence, conditional on tax-unit stabilization, QuickBooks retention and transparent Mailchimp economics. The weighted present model value exceeds the quote, but the bear outcome is adverse and source gaps prevent a validated target. Prioritize accountant/customer evidence of willingness to pay after AI automation. Review at tax seasons and comparable quarterly disclosures; reconsider if growth becomes solely price-led while customer losses persist.

Business quality, stewardship, structural opportunity and price are separate judgments. The Duan lens asks whether customers willingly return; Buffett emphasizes enduring cash economics; Munger directs attention to failure mechanisms; Li Lu asks whether productivity demand persists. These are analytical lenses, not simulated investor endorsements. Abundant public evidence does not establish economic certainty.
