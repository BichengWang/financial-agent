### Why customers pay
Advertisers pay to reach measurable intent and attention; consumers return for useful discovery/video; enterprises pay for reliable compute, productivity and security, while alternatives include other search engines, social/retail ads, AI assistants and competing clouds. [quarter] [filing]

## Inversion

### Strongest opposing thesis and falsifiable risks
The serious bear case is not that Google disappears. It is that it remains widely used while AI answers divert the highest-value transactions, make each response costlier and force investment whose returns accrue to users, chip/power suppliers or competitors.
This resembles earlier platform transitions in which engagement survived while the toll-taking layer moved; unlike a small legacy incumbent, Alphabet also owns distribution, models, video and enterprise infrastructure. Analogy is a failure mechanism, not a forecast.

| Risk | Mechanism/timing/severity | Early warning and falsifier | Mitigation / valuation treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI query substitution | 1–5 years; commercial intent routed away, higher inference cost; severe | Two successive quarters of weak Search growth despite rising query claims; persistent Services profit weakness | New ad formats/conversion quality; bear EPS contraction and 16x multiple |
| Capex overbuild/obsolescence | 1–5 years; cash leaves now, depreciation/demand disappoint later; severe | Spending guidance rises while cloud/Services profit and cash recovery stall | Flexible capacity/useful TPUs; lower growth/multiple, not a guessed maintenance-capex exemption |
| Cloud growth quality | Next several quarters; acquired/hardware revenue or concentrated capacity demand inflates recurring signal | Margin deterioration, slower customer consumption, weak backlog conversion | Broader workloads/security; base assumes growth moderates, bull requires durable conversion |
| Antitrust erosion | Ongoing; data sharing/default competition/adtech interoperability attack rents | Rival distribution adoption, publisher migration, worse auction economics | Product quality; bear multiple reflects lower durable rents; appeal/stay status unresolved |
| Financing/dilution | 1–3 years; common/preferred/ATM funds spending, share claims expand | Rising outstanding/conversion dilution with zero offsetting buybacks | Per-share model growth must be net of dilution; no automatic buyback credit |
| Governance/private marks | Persistent; controllers and mark-linked incentives favor empire building | Weak acquisition returns or expanded opaque related-party arrangements | No numerical guarantee; potential thesis veto if integrity evidence worsens |

## Industry

Durable demand is discovery, entertainment, enterprise computing and productivity. AI changes the interface and cost structure; demand growth need not leave the incumbent with the same toll rate.
The value chain runs from chips/power/data centers through models and cloud platforms to consumer/workplace interfaces, advertiser budgets and publisher/creator content. Google participates in many layers but competes for scarce power, capacity and talent. [quarter] [filing] [cnbc]
Search substitutes include AI assistants, retail search and social discovery; video rivals compete for finite attention; AWS/Azure and open-weight/self-hosted routes challenge Cloud/model prices. This competitor map is analytical context, not an observed market-share table.
Open-weight/token-cost pressure is specifically described in contemporaneous coverage; third-party capacity use can grow revenue while transferring economics to suppliers. [cnbc]
Search remedies prohibit exclusivity and require certain index/user data and syndication access. September 2026 adtech relief adds Prebid integrations, data export, nondiscriminatory bidding and six-year monitoring. These are distinct proceedings. [doj-search] [doj-adtech]
The observed government statements support behavioral relief, not an assumed breakup, completed appeal or confirmed current operational implementation. Legal execution details require further primary-case review.
Purchase commitments **$811B**, short-term **$200.7B**, and credit-backstop exposures highlight a future investment burden beyond cash capex alone. These are obligations/exposures, not all balance-sheet debt or a same-year cash spending forecast. [filing]
No independently defensible numerical TAM or market-capture forecast was established; avoid adding AI, cloud and advertising TAMs that overlap.
Li Lu's structural lens favors a lasting institution improving information productivity; it does not validate every incremental infrastructure dollar or give Alphabet exclusive ownership of the productivity surplus.
Bounded model growth therefore ranges from shrinking EPS to sustained high-teens per-share growth, with no assumption that Q2's 82% Cloud growth persists for five years.

## Decision

### Separate quality, certainty and price
Duan business lens: attractive core discovery/video/enterprise assets; medium-high confidence in utility, lower confidence in AI unit economics. A growing query count with falling profitable conversions would disconfirm capture.
Buffett durability lens: strong video/scale advantages, more contestable search interface and legally weakened distribution/adtech protections. Durable economics matter more than a temporary model lead.
Stewardship lens: ambitious useful reinvestment but a shift to issuance, suspended buybacks and controller/Bet incentives warrants discipline. Strong stock performance is not proof of allocation returns.
Munger inversion lens: the greatest error would be capitalizing mark-to-market earnings or treating capex as automatically high-return growth spending.
Li Lu structural lens: information/productivity demand persists; who captures AI's surplus is unresolved. Price lens: even the base scenario fails the chosen 10.5% price-only hurdle at this quote.
**Actionable research decision: watchlist, not currently an evidence-backed bargain.** Prioritize monetization/cash-conversion diligence rather than adopt the consensus target or make an allocation recommendation.

### Review gates and thesis breakers
- At the next earnings release (vendor schedules Oct 28), examine Search growth and Services profit alongside AI adoption; two weak quarters despite query growth would undermine monetization durability. [quote]
- Track Cloud margin/cash generation and separate TPU sales, Wiz contribution, rented capacity and recurring workloads before crediting current growth to a single sustainable cause.
- Reassess 2026 spending guidance, commitments and cash conversion; persistent capex escalation without per-share cash recovery is a bear trigger.
- Obtain current common/preferred conversion and ATM disclosures; share growth without comparable earnings-power growth breaks the dilution assumption.
- Confirm search/adtech appeal, stay and actual implementation terms from case documents; competitive effects can emerge after headline relief.
- Require credible incremental returns on infrastructure/acquisitions, not only backlog or model usage; seek enterprise workload renewal and advertiser conversion economics.
- Revisit pricing if a genuine discount to conservatively supported earning power appears; do not mechanically use $289.29 as a limit/order instruction.
A five-year market closure would be tolerable only if continued product utility, advertiser returns, enterprise retention and reinvestment returns are evidenced. This scan does not yet establish all four.
