## Moat

| Candidate | Evidence | Counterevidence / competitive response | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| EOS operational switching costs | Consistent Linux-based state-sharing architecture, telemetry, non-disruptive upgrades and CloudVision across domains | Open standards facilitate alternatives; white-box/open-source NOS can absorb engineering effort internally | Large customers shift production fabrics away without a reliability/cost penalty |
| Qualification/reliability know-how | Multi-generation cloud platforms and customer engineering collaboration | Supplier silicon is available to others; merchant ASIC access is not exclusive | Defect rates or job-completion performance lag bundled Nvidia/Cisco systems |
| Portfolio reuse | Campus/routing uses common operating model | Broad coverage adds testing/support complexity and lowers focus | Adjacencies grow while support costs erase margin advantage |

The moat is the software/qualification system, not an assumed monopoly over Ethernet ports. Arista identifies predominant switching-chip reliance on **Broadcom**, which is simultaneously a key enabler and a potential bargaining bottleneck [issuer-10k]. Longstanding revenue growth supports execution confidence, not a quantified price/retention moat. Ten-year durability is **medium**, contingent on maintaining system-level utility when ASIC generations and optics change; no numerical NPS score or churn assertion is inferred from promotional language.

## Inversion

| Failure path | Timing / severity | Warning | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| A cloud titan reallocates fabric orders | Next budget cycle; major revenue/margin hit | Concentration rises while bookings or acceptance delays worsen | Bear 5% EPS growth and 24x exit; full customer loss can be worse |
| Nvidia vertically bundles proprietary/better Ethernet fabrics | Current to five years; share and pricing erosion | Production wins favor bundled GPU/network economics | Base growth requires retained open-fabric share, not just AI capex |
| Broadcom allocation/pricing or design access bottleneck | Product-cycle transition; delivery and margin disruption | ASIC lead times widen, substitutes need redesign | Unmodeled extreme supply interruption |
| EOS advantage commoditizes | Several refresh cycles; persistent rent loss | Open NOS achieves same reliability with lower all-in cost | Lower bear multiple rather than pretending a permanent software annuity |
| Acceptance cash timing creates false confidence | Quarterly; quality-of-earnings surprise | Deferred revenue and deferred costs diverge, receivables age | OCF is not normalized owner earnings |

The strongest bear argument is that hyperscaler network demand grows yet procurement internalizes the software value and upstream ASICs retain the scarce rent. This is stronger than assuming traffic disappears. Avoid anchoring to an AI label or a consensus target; look for customer-level production evidence that contradicts the moat. No invented probability is assigned to geopolitical or security disasters.

## Industry

[INFERENCE] Durable distributed computing requires reliable data movement; the number of dollars accruing to a switch vendor depends on architecture, software and buyer bargaining power. Arista sits between Broadcom merchant ASICs/foundry-linked supply and cloud/enterprise infrastructure buyers. Cisco, HPE/Juniper, Nvidia and white-box NOS are named alternatives; customers can disaggregate procurement or develop internal networks [issuer-10k].
Scale-out Ethernet is an opportunity, not proof that scale-up proprietary NVLink disappears. The filing calls open ESUN scale-up a future incremental opportunity and documents competition from InfiniBand/NVLink sold with Nvidia GPUs [issuer-10k]. Copper, pluggable optics, linear pluggable optics and co-packaged optics change reach/power/serviceability economics. The 7060XE7 LPO claim of about 60% interconnect-power reduction is issuer-reported, not an independently measured fleet saving [issuer-q2]. [INFERENCE] LPO/CPO can remove standalone DSP content or move value into packaging and system qualification; Arista must integrate the winning route rather than assume transceiver spending itself is its revenue.
No numerical independent TAM or precise Ethernet share forecast was retained. Power, cooling, acceptance testing and GPU supply limit deployment; AI capex growth alone does not justify proportional EPS growth. Scenario growth below is intentionally slower than recent revenue growth to reflect pricing, concentration and maturity.

## Decision

**Watchlist; understandable and high-quality business, unattractive modeled cushion on the retained historical-capacity basis.** Buffett-style durability rests on operational reliability rather than port monopoly; Duan’s business test is stronger than the verified people/incentive test; Munger inversion highlights customers and suppliers holding bargaining power; Li Lu’s structural demand can benefit the ecosystem without accruing fully to Arista.
Review actual recurring service renewals, customer win/loss and acceptance cycles; separate cloud-titan growth from campus diversification; track GAAP gross margin through 1.6T transitions and inventory/deferred costs. Thesis breaks if EOS loses operational value, top customers move enduring volume to substitutes, or supplier dependence prevents competitive shipments. Obtain current primary share count, customer ROI and incentive details before a stronger price or stewardship conclusion. A five-year exchange closure would leave an understandable installed-network business, but concentration risk and entry valuation do not disappear.
