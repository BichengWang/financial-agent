## Inversion

The strongest bear thesis is that AI increases activity while eliminating paid seats and employee-equity costs consume the resulting platform cash. A profitable Q4 does not establish durable annual profitability.

| Failure mechanism | Timing / severity | Falsifying evidence | Valuation consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agent workflows reduce human seats | Next renewals; structural | More MCP/Rovo use but lower paid seats or total contracted wallet | No current EPS-multiple target |
| Migration/accounting reverses growth | FY2027–FY2029; material | Data Center decline exceeds orderly cloud conversion; receivable growth persists | Downside assessed through lower sustainable cash, not annualizing Q4 |
| SBC consumes cash yield | Compensation cycles; persistent | Dilution/buybacks fail to improve GAAP profit per share | Historical FCF is not owner earnings |
| Acquisition/integration disappointment | Several years | Weak acquired engagement, goodwill impairment, rising service burden | Avoid a precision terminal multiple |
| Governance or security failure | Discrete; severe | Unresolved control/related-party issues or permission breach | Outside ordinary operating scenario assumptions |

Bias checks: do not anchor to prior peak stock prices, adjusted forwardPE38.11 or1mMAU. Evidence that breaks the cautious thesis would be multiple annual periods of GAAP profitability, reduced SBC burden and paid AI expansion after accounting for seat substitution. Evidence that breaks the favorable business thesis would be context access migrating off-platform without corresponding monetization.

## Industry

Value chain: developers/service teams → work-tracking and knowledge systems → agents/connectors → cloud inference and human execution. Durable demand is coordinated work and governance, not a particular issue-tracker interface. Atlassian's permissioned context may remain valuable when agents perform tasks; openness can simultaneously expand reach and reduce control of pricing. [TEAM-primary]

The shareholder letter discusses Data Center end-of-life inMarch2029 and effects of term-license recognition following theSeptember2025 announcement. Thus FY2026 growth is not automatically a permanent run rate. FY2027 guide13% total growth versus25.5% cloud and−17%Data Center explicitly embeds a transition. [TEAM-letter] [TEAM-primary]

Competition spans developer platforms, enterprise service procurement and lightweight collaboration/AI applications. Enterprise isolated deployments and forward-deployed support raise costs; AI supplier charges may move reinvestment into operating expense. No independent TAM is used. Bear economics involve seat substitution and weaker cash conversion; base requires orderly migration and annual GAAP profitability; upside requires paid agents/collections growing the total wallet rather than repackaging unchanged budgets. These are business conditions, not numeric share-price forecasts.

## Decision

Watchlist only. The recurring work-context business deserves product/customer diligence, but no validated target or definitive allocation follows from adjusted profits. Review conversion of paid seats into AI consumption, collections after migration, annual GAAP profitability and founder governance. A five-year market closure would be supportable only with cash-producing customer relationships after employee equity and integration costs—not a multiple of selectively adjusted EPS.

Business quality, stewardship, structural opportunity and price are separate judgments. The Duan lens asks whether customers willingly return; Buffett emphasizes enduring cash economics; Munger directs attention to failure mechanisms; Li Lu asks whether productivity demand persists. These are analytical lenses, not simulated investor endorsements. Abundant public evidence does not establish economic certainty.
