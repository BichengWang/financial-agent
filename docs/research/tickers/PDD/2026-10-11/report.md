## Moat

The plausible moat is a combination of merchant density, consumer traffic, price discovery and China supply-chain access. More consumers attract merchants; merchant breadth and lower prices reinforce consumer use. The monetization model supports low reported fixed-asset cash intensity relative to retail revenue, though advertising, subsidies, fulfillment/compliance support and merchant economics are substantial operating costs. The annual report explicitly says the two platforms share value propositions/operational model, but does not disclose a fully segregated Temu profitability/cash-flow account. [issuer-annual; issuer-interim]

A low acquisition price is not itself durable moat evidence. Competitors can match discounts; merchants can multi-home; overseas delivery, returns, product safety and localization can erode contribution economics. The correct test is sustainable margin/cash generation after compliance and ecosystem investments, not gross merchandise activity or a blended consolidated P/E. [issuer-annual]

## Inversion

The thesis fails if apparent low multiples reflect structurally lower sustainable profit: merchant monetization hits a ceiling, price competition raises incentives, trust/safety spending becomes persistent, or international tariffs/customs, de-minimis rules, product-safety/data regulations and consumer protection materially impair Temu economics. The annual report describes the risks of operating across changing jurisdictions and the possibility contractualVIE arrangements become unenforceable, potentially renderingADS interests worthless. These are disclosed legal risks, not a current delisting claim. [issuer-annual]

PCAOB/HFCAA risk must be stated historically and conditionally: inspection access was restored in2022; that is not evidence of an active2026 trading prohibition. A sustained decline in attributable profits despite revenue growth, restricted/merchant funds misread as netcash, or missing separate international profitability disclosures would invalidate an easy 'cash bargain' thesis. Latest quarterlyprofit already fell12% despite8%revenuegrowth. [issuer-annual; issuer-interim]

## Industry

Pinduoduo competes with Alibaba, JD and other Chinese traffic/commerce platforms; Temu competes across international marketplaces and retailers under jurisdiction-specific cost structures. Inventory-light merchant services should not be compared mechanically with JD's gross merchandise retail revenue or with Alibaba's cloud revenue. Regulation touches customs, consumer safety, data and merchant conduct; enforcement/compliance can affect both revenue recognition and costs. [issuer-annual]

The consolidated accounts do not independently identify a durable profitable Temu unit, geographic revenue quality or clean distributable excess cash. Current June-quarter governance/trust language and net-income pressure indicate that compliance and merchant support are economic inputs, not peripheral risk boilerplate. [issuer-interim]
