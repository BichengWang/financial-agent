## Business

Data centers, cloud operators, device makers and industrial customers pay for DRAM, HBM and NAND because compute requires bandwidth/capacity and durable storage. Memory is necessary infrastructure, but capacity additions and standardized portions of products make prices cyclical. HBM customization/qualification can improve customer stickiness without abolishing rival capacity.

## Industry

Upstream equipment, advanced packaging, wafer capacity and process yields determine memory supply; accelerated-compute/cloud spending and devices determine bits demanded. AI broadens bandwidth/storage demand, but the industry's response is very large capital expansion by all suppliers [issuer-call], [issuer-latest]. HBM can command differentiated economics while NAND/standardDRAM remain more exposed to standardized pricing. [INFERENCE] Model efficiency might lower bits per task yet increase affordable total tasks; its net effect on Micron pricing is uncertain. No numeric TAM extrapolates issuer record-quarter demand, and no independent market-share statistic is invented. Social utility of memory/compute is substantial without resolving the correct public-equity entry price.

## Decision

**Watchlist, cycle-normalization prerequisite before price target.** Duan: products meet durable needs; Buffett: differentiatedHBM improves economics but memory-pricing cycles challenge durability; stewardship: validate capex discipline over a downturn; Munger: real record earnings can be the worst valuation anchor; Li Lu: AI infrastructure matters socially while scarce-product rents may migrate to customers.

Review actual contract floor economics, net deposits/refunds, inventory/pricing trends, qualification yields, FY2027 capital execution and share-adjusted cash profitability. Thesis break: normalized pricing cannot support returns on newly built capacity, or proprietaryHBM advantages vanish while commitments persist. Confidence high on latest audited numbers, medium on operating announcements, low on sustainable margin/EPS. No confident target or allocation is presented; omission is substantive risk control rather than unfinished work.
