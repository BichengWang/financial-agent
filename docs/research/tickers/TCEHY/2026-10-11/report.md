## Business

Tencent monetizes a communications ecosystem through games/content subscriptions, advertising, payments and enterprise services. FY2025 revenue was **RMB751.766B**: VAS RMB369.281B, Marketing Services RMB144.973B and FinTech/Business Services RMB229.435B. Domestic games reached RMB164.2B and international games RMB77.4B; social networks RMB127.7B. FY2025 gross margin increased to 56% from 53%, and equity-holder attributable IFRS earnings were RMB224.842B. These are operating segment/reporting-currency facts, not translated USD segment statements. [issuer-annual]

FY2025 operating cash flow was **RMB303.052B**; cash PP&E/prepayment/construction/investment-property spending was **RMB87.482B**. The engine's capital-spend input intentionally excludes RMB25.399B cash intangible purchases and lease payments. Headline capital-expenditure additions of RMB79.198B are a different accrual/additions measure and are not used as cash spend. Native OCF-minus-this-capex is therefore a narrow cash-generation proxy, **not owner earnings or Tencent's adjusted free-cash-flow definition**. Cash/equivalents RMB141.041B excludes restricted cash RMB6.977B and term deposits. Borrowings plus notes were RMB387.733B, excluding RMB18.666B leases. [issuer-annual]

The latest primary quarter is important, not buried behind the stronger annual cash flow: **2Q2026 revenue RMB204.785B (+11%), IFRS attributable profit RMB56.022B (+0.7%), diluted EPS RMB6.104**. Marketing grew 22%, while international-game revenue declined 0.8% in reported FX (up4% constant currency). Tencent disclosed **negative adjusted FCF RMB13.8B**, with OCF RMB52.7B overwhelmed by capital-expenditure payments RMB59.3B, content RMB5.0B and leases RMB2.2B. ## Moat

Weixin/WeChat combines identity, social graph, messaging, payments, Mini Programs, Mini Shops and content in one frequently used surface. Combined MAU was 1.418B at December2025 and 1.439B at June2026. These distribution/network effects lower the cost of introducing monetization features; durable game franchises reinforce cash generation. June-quarter marketing growth reflects AI recommendation, AIM+ campaign tools and closed-loop commerce. [issuer-annual; issuer-interim]

The moat is strongest in communications/distribution, not automatically in every AI model or cloud workload. Video/social competition, ad-load limits, user fatigue and changing game tastes can constrain monetization without destroying usage. A cloud business achieving management-reported adjusted operating profitability does not establish attractive returns on newly purchased GPU capacity. The June cash outflow is the explicit falsification test for a simplistic 'AI only raises margins' story. [issuer-interim; issuer-annual]

## Inversion

The thesis fails if: (1) the social graph remains large but ad/payment conversion loses pricing power; (2) game renewals/new releases cannot replenish franchise cash; (3) AI compute, content and inference cash costs grow faster than monetization; (4) PRC regulation restricts content, games, fintech/data use or the contractual structure; or (5) receipt liquidity, depositary actions or geopolitics impair U.S. shareholder realizability despite healthy operating subsidiaries. Tencent's Structure Contract disclosures expressly describe legal/enforceability uncertainty. [issuer-annual; issuer-interim; citi-adr]

Monitor recurring IFRS and non-IFRS reconciliations, net cash after compute commitments, cash capital payments rather than additions, ad growth versus time spent, overseas-game reported/constant-currency growth, and receipt terms. Investment gains and associate losses can distort IFRS EPS; persistently negative cash generation would refute an earnings-multiple-only valuation even if EPS remains positive.

## Industry

Tencent competes for entertainment/advertising attention with short-video and other platforms, for games with domestic/global publishers, and for enterprise AI/cloud workloads with Alibaba and other hyperscalers. Payments are regulated infrastructure, not unrestricted software. PRC game approvals/minor-protection rules, antitrust/data regulation and export restrictions on advanced compute affect different earnings lines through different mechanisms. These are issuer-described risk categories, not a claim that a specific unobserved enforcement action or imminent delisting exists. [issuer-annual]

The strategic strength is ecosystem distribution plus durable franchises; the strategic danger is using that cash engine to subsidize unconstrained infrastructure/model/application investment. June2026 separates operational growth from investment cash costs clearly enough to make the tension measurable. [issuer-interim]
