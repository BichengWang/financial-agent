## Business

Building owners, distributors, contractors and transport operators pay Carrier for HVAC, controls, maintenance and cold-chain equipment that preserve comfort, uptime and cargo; replacement/servicing recurs but new construction and distributor inventory remain cyclical [issuer-fy25, issuer-q226, sa-quote]. An installed chiller has specification/service switching friction, whereas commodity residential units still face channel competition.

### Backlog versus realized margins/cash
Commercial HVAC Q4 2025 orders rose nearly 50%, but Americas residential sales fell 38%; FY2025 total sales declined 3%, GAAP operating margin was 10.0% versus adjusted 15.1% [issuer-fy25]. Q2 2026 company orders rose roughly 40%, commercial about 65% and data-center orders over 300%; nevertheless Americas commercial sales fell 8% on delivery timing, GAAP operating margin fell to 13.0% from 14.8%, and adjusted margin to 17.2% from 19.1%. Mix and input costs can absorb pricing despite an order surge [issuer-q226]. Orders may cancel without penalty: backlog is demand visibility, not guaranteed cash or a take-or-pay moat.

FY2025 consolidated OCF $2.513B included $424M discontinued operating cash; continuing OCF was $2.089B. The native $2.121B FCF convention uses consolidated OCF, while continuing-only cash economics are less favorable and not silently substituted [issuer-fy25]. H1 2026 OCF was $1.006B, capex $211M and release FCF $795M versus $988M prior H1. June receivables/inventory increased from December levels while cash fell to $1.344B. Q2 strength alone does not establish full-year cash conversion [issuer-q226]. No exact customer-concentration percentage is sourced; distributors and large institutional orders create both channel and project concentration even across diversified geographies.

## Decision

**Watchlist, provisional; business more attractive than the conservative price case.** Understandable recurring customer need and service opportunities satisfy the “right business” lens with moderate confidence. Durability and stewardship are qualified by channel cycles, debt and transaction comparisons; record orders are not investment certainty. Native scenario timing is a disclosed convention, not source truth or approved future returns.

Review triggers: sustained delivery conversion, continuing-only cash generation, residential distributor normalization, profitable refrigerant transitions, VCS integration and disciplined debt/capital returns. Thesis breaks if backlog fails to deliver margins or recurrent portfolio adjustments are required to present attractive earnings. No validated allocation or guaranteed target is offered; a five-year closure would require confidence in service durability and balance-sheet stewardship, not simply the data-center story.
