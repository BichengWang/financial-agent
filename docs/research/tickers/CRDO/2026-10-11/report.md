## Business

Credo sells reliable, energy-efficient connectivity that minimizes link failures and moves data across short copper and optical links. Hyperscalers/OEMs buy cable reliability, power/space efficiency, signal integrity and qualification convenience; alternatives include passive copper, other AEC vendors, optical modules, customer-specific designs and integrated silicon from Broadcom/Marvell [issuer-10k].

| Product / buyer utility | Economics | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| ZeroFlap active electrical cables | SerDes, retimers, cable/module integration and diagnostics; more integrated value than a bare chip | Reach is limited; next-generation signal loss and customer architecture can move links to optics |
| Optical DSP/components/transceivers | PAM/coherent connectivity, SiPho PICs, module DSPs and ZF optical products | Competition and LRO/LPO/CPO can remove specific component functions |
| OmniConnect / memory / chip-to-chip | Additional within-system interconnect content | Announced adjacency is not verified high-volume margin or TAM |
| PILOT diagnostics / IP | Software-enabled reliability and SerDes expertise | Software utility does not automatically equal recurring subscription revenue |

## Moat

| Candidate | Support | Competitive counterweight | Falsifier |

Distinguish **contract customers** from end users. FY2026 contract customer A is 49% and B 32%; top ten contract customers total 90%. Ten-year durability is **medium-low to medium**, conditional on repeatedly retaining efficient/reliable links as reach and packaging change. Design qualification is a generation-level moat; the installed base does not create an assumed social/network effect. Recent 68% gross margin is evidence of current value capture, not proof of ten-year pricing permanence. No independent customer ROI, outage-frequency or retention data was retained.

| Larger competitors compress ASPs | Refresh cycle; sustained margin pressure | Gross margin falls without compensating volume/cost gains | Lower exit multiple reflects rent erosion |
| Foundry/manufacturing interruption | Immediate to multiquarter; shipment disruption | Capacity/yield problems with limited alternate sourcing | Unmodeled tail; TSMC-only annual wafer sourcing is material |

The strongest bear is real AI connectivity growth with customers retaining the architecture choice and alternative vendors capturing the next-generation sockets. Credo can be technologically strong today without owning a permanent toll bridge. FY2026 sourcing exclusively from TSMC and few manufacturers, cancellable customer orders and absent long-term customer purchase commitments magnify the asymmetry [issuer-10k]. Avoid treating purple cable branding or management's failure-free language as independent proof of an invulnerable moat.

## Industry

[INFERENCE] Electrical links usually avoid optical-conversion complexity at practical short reach; signal loss and speed constrain them. Credo describes AECs up to about 7m, optical DSP use from about 5m to more than 10km, and claims roughly 50% less power than optical solutions and 75% less bulk than passive DAC alternatives for relevant cables [issuer-10k]. These are vendor claims tied to configurations, not universal comparable fleet tests.
Bluebird 3nm 200G/lane DSP targets 1.6T full-retimed/LRO optics; LRO removes receiver DSP function, so optics growth does not translate one-for-one into traditional DSP content [issuer-10k]. Broadcom's integrated switching/CPO/SerDes offerings and Marvell's custom/photonic portfolio can compete or influence where the profit pool lands. Astera and cable/module suppliers are additional disclosed competitive alternatives. A mature-node advantage in some Credo products must not be generalized to all products: Bluebird's disclosed 3nm implementation contradicts that blanket assertion.
PILOT/link diagnostics and qualified complete cable systems may retain value independent of copper versus optics, but optical packaging, replacement/serviceability, thermal budgets and customer standardization decide deployment. No numeric independent TAM, exact market share, ASP curve or “all hyperscalers standardized” claim is supported. Five years spans several interconnect refreshes, making persistent growth a demanding assumption rather than continuation of one customer's current ramp.

## Decision

**Watchlist; compelling current product utility, insufficient price cushion and independent durability evidence.** Buffett's cost/reliability utility lens favors understanding qualified systems; Duan's people test requires evidence of disciplined dilution/acquisition choices; Munger inversion highlights end-user concentration and substitution; Li Lu's data-movement runway must be separated from who earns its rents.
Track contract versus true end-user concentration separately; current primary ordinary-share and acquired-asset financing terms; link-error/reliability evidence from customers; AEC-to-optics socket retention, gross-margin trend and inventory/receivable conversion; incremental cash per common share after SBC/dilution. Break the thesis if large customers replace qualified links with cheaper/better alternatives, if technology adjacencies fail qualification while ownership costs compound, or if cash per share persistently trails headline adjusted profits. A five-year market closure would expose an understandable but concentrated component franchise, not make today's premium safe.
