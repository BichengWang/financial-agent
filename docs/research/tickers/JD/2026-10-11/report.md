## Business

JD combines direct merchandise retail, marketplace/services and logistics, with food delivery, Joybuy and Jingxi among its new ventures. FY2025 revenue **RMB1,309.085B/USD187.197B grew 13%**. Ordinary-attributable net income **RMB19.631B/USD2.807B** differs from consolidated net RMB23.142B/USD3.309B. Gross merchandise revenue carries procurement, inventory, fulfillment and labor costs; it is not mechanically comparable with PDD's merchant-service revenue. [issuer-annual]

The retail core performed well: annual JD Retail operating income **RMB51.402B/USD7.350B**, margin 4.6% versus 4.0%. But new businesses lost **RMB46.641B/USD6.670B**, leaving group operating income only **RMB2.774B/USD0.397B**. This is a capital-allocation question, not proof the retail operation lacks strengths. [issuer-annual]

Annual OCF was **USD2.716B**. Annual MD&A capital expenditure is rounded **RMB17.6B/USD2.5B**, including infrastructure/development/land. Issuer adjusted FCF **USD0.926B** uses net capex USD1.821B and consumer-financing adjustments, unlike the native unadjusted OCF-minus-rounded-gross-capex formula. Development-property cash can also pass through operating accounts; the crude native proxy is not owner cash flow. Cash/equivalents **USD19.660B** excludes restricted cash and short-term investments. Estimated debt **USD10.581B** sums separately reported bank/senior components and excludes leases USD4.734B. [issuer-annual]

Latest **2Q2026** revenue RMB346.4B fell 2.9%, while operating income recovered to RMB4.5B (1.3% margin) from a prior-year loss. Diluted ADS EPS was RMB5.01/USD0.74. Retail income RMB13.5B carried 4.6% margin. New-business losses narrowed; however fulfillment expenses rose 10.4% to RMB24.5B, reaching 7.1% of revenue versus 6.2%; R&D rose 37.7% while marketing fell 24.8%. Loss reduction is encouraging, not proof expansion is self-funding. [issuer-interim]

## Moat

Authenticity, procurement/warehouse execution and reliable delivery are valuable in branded, electronics and home-goods categories. Integrated logistics and enterprise procurement support trust, scale and efficiency. The latest issuer disclosure cites CHANEL and Costco partnerships as brand-access examples, not independent proof of permanent dominance. [issuer-interim; issuer-annual]

Physical execution can be valuable and capital/labor intensive simultaneously. Price competition, inventory obsolescence and service investment constrain cash conversion. Core retail advantages do not automatically transfer to food delivery or European direct retail. Watch retail margin, inventory/payables, cash capital spending and venture loss reduction rather than group revenue alone. [issuer-annual; issuer-interim]

## Management

Founder Richard Qiangdong Liu's Class B control is material: **73.1% aggregate voting power at March 18, 2026**, including 3.6% exercisable for Fortune Rising. Voting control is not an economic ownership percentage. Outside Class A/ADS investors have limited power over expansion and allocation choices. [issuer-annual; issuer-interim]

JD repurchased approximately **69.9M Class A/34.9M ADS equivalents for USD1.0B** in first-half 2026; remaining authorization was USD1.0B at June 30. Buybacks and the USD1/ADS annual dividend are positive distribution evidence but must be weighed against venture losses and capital spending. Net shares after reserved/repurchased class scopes are more informative than gross repurchases. [issuer-interim; issuer-annual]

## Inversion

The thesis fails if stronger retail margins cannot offset persistently funded venture losses, fulfillment costs outrun monetization, inventory/pricing/credit cycles erode cash, or food-delivery/European expansion requires indefinite subsidies. Healthy retail can still be a poor group investment if cash is redeployed outside its moat. Fiscal 2025 venture losses nearly consumed retail operating profit, a sourced downside mechanism rather than generic risk language. [issuer-annual]

VIE enforceability, PRC data/platform/labor/competition rules, foreign trade obligations and dual-class minority protection are separate risks. Latest loss narrowing does not eliminate them. Monitor cash conversion after consumer-financing/property adjustments, leases and cash capex, retail inventory efficiency, and new-business cash losses rather than adjusted EPS alone. [issuer-annual; issuer-interim]

## Industry

JD competes with Alibaba, PDD and others in Chinese commerce, with specialist logistics companies in fulfillment and established local networks in food delivery. Each arena has different acquisition, capital and regulatory costs. Large direct-retail gross revenue is not higher-quality revenue merely because it exceeds merchant-platform net fees. [issuer-annual]

June services revenue grew 6.8% while product revenue fell 5.4%; mix supported retail promotional-season margin while product demand/high-base effects constrained growth. Higher fulfillment spending and new-venture infrastructure are the economic counterweights. [issuer-interim]
