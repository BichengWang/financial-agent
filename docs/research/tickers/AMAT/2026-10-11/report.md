## Business

Chipmakers pay Applied Materials for deposition, etch, implant, polishing and inspection systems that improve transistor performance and manufacturing yield, and they return for parts, upgrades and service on installed tools; alternatives include competing process tools and different manufacturing recipes. Value is materials engineering and process integration, not owning semiconductor unit demand [issuer-annual], [issuer-update].

Rivals can win particular recipes even in a growing semiconductor market. Export compliance tails and Taiwan manufacturing concentration are not probabilistically priced by three smooth EPS scenarios. Anchoring on an AI narrative or the strongest quarter is avoided.

## Industry

Applied sits upstream of foundries and memory manufacturers, competing with Lam Research, Tokyo Electron and specialist vendors across process steps rather than replacing lithography with a single universal tool. Smaller devices and 3D integration require more materials/process complexity; HBM and advanced packaging add tool intensity, but tool spending is constrained by customer funding, fab readiness, yield learning and geopolitics [issuer-update].

FY2025 Systems mix67%foundry/logic,26%DRAM,7%flash and30%consolidated China sales quantify cyclic/export exposure. AGS softens but does not eliminate the WFE cycle. No independently sourced WFE market forecast or numerical TAM is supplied. Secular complexity justifies bounded growth assumptions, not immunity to a downcycle.

## Decision

Watchlist: attractive materials-engineering franchise, but current quote needs much stronger per-share capture or a more persistent premium multiple than this conservative capacity model provides. Review annualFY2026 cash conversion, new qualification wins, EPIC economics, China permissions, capex productivity and buyback discipline. Thesis breaks if WFE growth fails to reach Applied, export access shrinks permanently, or services are less resilient than expected.

Duan's business/people lens separates customer usefulness from stewardship; Buffett's durability lens asks whether the franchise and cash survive a market closure; Munger's inversion emphasizes specific failure mechanisms; Li Lu's structural lens asks who captures technology gains. These are analytical lenses, not simulated endorsements. Confidence is moderate in observed financial extraction and low-to-moderate in conditional future value. Abundant consensus coverage cannot overcome unknown customer, legal or supply-chain terms.
