## Business

Equipment makers, semiconductor fabs and electronics manufacturers pay MKS for instruments, vacuum/power/control subsystems, photonics and specialty chemicals that enable demanding processes; they return for qualified components, services and consumables because changing recipes can threaten yield. Alternatives are rival subsystems, toolmaker in-sourcing and competing surface-processing chemistry [issuer-annual], [issuer-update].

The strongest bear case is that useful, indispensable inputs still have cyclical OEM customers and acquisition debt, so an AI equipment upcycle is a poor time to capitalize peak earnings at a premium multiple. A cash-generative business can be a fragile equity.

## Industry

MKS occupies the component, precision-instrument, photonics and surface-chemistry layers beneath equipment OEMs and chip/electronics producers. Process complexity and advanced packaging expand technical demands; OEM concentration creates bargaining pressure, while fab capex cycles transmit quickly into component orders. Electronics chemistry can produce repeat consumption but depends on production volumes and environmental compliance.

Export/trade restrictions, international suppliers and FX can affect both demand and cost.
