## Business

### Value-chain exposure, not a semiconductor manufacturer
Investors pay 0.33% annually for a tradable basket of U.S.-listed semiconductor-sector equities, rather than choose and rebalance individual chip/equipment firms. The current benchmark is the **NYSE Semiconductor Index**, not the PHLX index suggested by the legacy product-page URL. The July 31,2026 prospectus selects the 30 largest eligible U.S.-listed semiconductor companies subject to classification, capitalization and liquidity criteria and permits representative sampling [prospectus].

The October 9 product page has 30 holdings. October 8 exposure is semiconductors 82.23%, semiconductor equipment 17.62% and cash/derivatives 0.15% [sponsor]. [INFERENCE] Designers, memory producers, manufacturers and equipment suppliers have different economics but often share end-market demand and capacity cycles. Diversifying among them does not remove the semiconductor cycle.

Five-year fund NAV calendar returns were 2021+44.00%,2022−35.03%,2023+66.90%,2024+12.97%,2025+40.71% [factsheet]. They are historical net-fee total-return observations, not corporate sales growth or forecast CAGR. The 2022 decline despite an enduring chip industry is useful counterevidence to a smooth secular-compounding narrative.

## Industry

The fund reaches electronic-material manufacturers, LED/OLED businesses and semiconductor-associated equipment/services including packaging/testing under the prospectus classification [prospectus]. [INFERENCE] Growing digital infrastructure can create durable demand while competition pushes economic rent toward a few architecture/process bottlenecks or customers. Equipment's profits depend on customers' investment budgets; memory price recovery is not the same economic engine as proprietary software ecosystems.

Current sponsor holding P/E 51.42 on October 8 is a portfolio statistic; its breadth cannot be equated to a single business at that multiple [sponsor]. The June factsheet glossary excludes negative earnings/extraordinary items and caps individual P/E inputs above 60; aggregation definitions matter [factsheet]. No unsupported semiconductor TAM or aggregate EPS CAGR is introduced. A broad index or different semiconductor fund is a substitute with different concentration/value-chain exposure, not necessarily equivalent economics.
