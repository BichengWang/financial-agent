## Business

Customers pay Nebius for GPU capacity, cloud orchestration and production inference because reliable, quickly provisioned clusters can shorten model-development and deployment cycles; hyperscalers, other neoclouds and owned clusters are alternatives. This is a capital-intensive service, not an asset-light software subscription by default [nbis-q2, nbis-letter].

| Driver | Evidence | Economic interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| AI cloud capacity | Q2 cloud revenue $574.9m; group $582.3m; cloud adjusted EBITDA $285.7m/49.7% [nbis-letter] | Scale is monetizing, but EBITDA excludes depreciation and stock compensation |
| Contract mix | Short 3–6 months, core 1–3 years, long investment-grade contracts [nbis-letter] | Short deals reprice faster; long deals finance assets but cap upside and concentrate risk |
| Inference/software | Token Factory production workloads more than tripled Q2; Eigen AI/Clarifai acquisitions [nbis-letter] | Operational differentiation possible; workloads are not a separately verified profit pool |
| Other assets | Avride, TripleTen; minority ClickHouse/Toloka stakes [nbis-q2] | Different risk/cash profiles; minority mark-ups are not cloud customer revenue |

Legacy Russian search/advertising earnings and proceeds are not NBIS's continuing cloud franchise. H1 OCF includes $4.395b deferred-revenue growth while cash capex reached $8.1303b. Prepayments finance delivery obligations; they are not free distributable earnings. ## Inversion

The strongest bear case is that revenue and adjusted EBITDA scale while replacement GPUs, debt service and delivery obligations prevent durable owner cash generation.

| Failure mechanism | Timing / severity | Warning / mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity misses customer milestones | 2026–27; contract penalties or lost utilization | Track energization and tranche acceptance, not contracted GW; stage asset deployment |
| Prepayment dependence reverses | Next delivery/renewal cycles; liquidity squeeze | Deferred-revenue inflow slows while capex persists; maintain unencumbered liquidity |
| GPU obsolescence/pricing correction | 1–5 years; impaired residual assets | Older-generation hourly rates fall and useful lives prove too long; diversify workloads |
| Concentration/default | Multi-year; severe | Monitor Meta/Microsoft acceptance and payments; undisclosed termination provisions remain unknown |
| Debt/dilution snowball | Continuous; permanent per-share impairment | ATM usage, warrant conversion, liens and interest grow faster than earning capacity |

No invented catastrophe probabilities or valuation-risk premium hides these discontinuities. Infrastructure booms are a useful analogy for traffic growth without investor returns, not proof of failure. Avoid anchoring to Yandex profits, ClickHouse valuation headlines, annualized monthly revenue or the current consensus target.

## Industry

[INFERENCE] Training, inference and agentic applications create enduring compute demand; the uncertain question is who captures its surplus. NVIDIA supplies accelerators, power providers/sites enable deployment, Nebius orchestrates compute, customers monetize applications, and creditors fund assets. Hyperscalers and specialized clouds compete on delivered performance and availability; customer-owned and alternative-accelerator systems are substitutes. Efficiency gains can reduce cost per token while expanding total workloads, but neither direction guarantees revenue or margins.

Meta's March 16 agreement is **$12b dedicated capacity** beginning early 2027 plus **up to $15b** additional available capacity over five years, with Nebius initially intending third-party sales and Meta taking remaining capacity. “Up to $27b” must not be treated as unconditional current annual revenue [nbis-meta]. Management reports over $40b customer commitments and expects over $9b 2026 prepayments; these are different concepts and dates, not additive cash forecasts [nbis-letter]. Contracted power differs from connected/installed capacity. No independent numeric TAM, utilization distribution or residual-GPU-price curve is retained.

## Decision

**Watchlist, not validated price attractiveness.** Business demand is well evidenced; durable shareholder cash economics and contract downside are not. Duan's right-business test is partly met by reliable compute service; Buffett-style durable pricing power is unproven; stewardship shows execution but aggressive financing; Munger-style inversion centers on asset obsolescence and prepaid obligations; Li Lu's productivity lens supports demand, not a guaranteed equity return.

Review triggers: commissioned capacity reconciles to sales; older GPU rates hold after new supply; cash flows remain adequate as prepayment growth normalizes; share/warrant/debt burdens stop outrunning recurring operating earnings; useful-life changes match realized replacement cycles. Thesis breaks if contract delivery failures or impaired collateral force financing on unfavorable terms. AI confidence is moderate for retained disclosures, lower for unit economics; economic certainty is low despite rich public coverage. A five-year market closure would require financing resilience, not merely a revenue backlog.
