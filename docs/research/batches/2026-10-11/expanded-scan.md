## Targets and horizon interpretation

The central index has **34 conditional discounted earnings-multiple targets** and **16 intentional model omissions**. Those targets are present-value comparisons, not forecast future sale prices or validated fair values. Scenario terminal prices and price-only CAGR remain in each report and calculation file, distinct from EPS-growth assumptions, weighted present value and observed YoY returns.

For the **25 newly modeled companies**, the calendar conventions are explicit assumptions:

| Assumed holding/growth years | Assumed terminal date | Newly modeled companies |
|---:|---|---:|
| 3 | 2029-10-11 | 6 |
| 4 | 2030-10-11 | 1 |
| 5 | 2031-10-11 | 18 |

Starting earning capacity may be a historical GAAP/IFRS proxy or an expressly assumed normalized amount. These are not factual current-earnings bridges. The index labels the anchor's kind so an assumption cannot masquerade as reported earnings. Terminal multiples and the fiscal/run-rate alignment are disclosed per report; growth, probabilities and hurdles are subjective sensitivity inputs, not calibrated distributions.

The **nine preserved initial company models** still configure five years without a verified calendar/EPS alignment. Their unresolved timing remains visible; preservation is not a retroactive endorsement of their dates. See the [initial nine-ticker return audit](return-audit.md).

- **SPY, QQQ, SOXX, XBI, TLT:** operating-company EPS models are inapplicable. Completed fund valuation sections analyze NAV/price, mandate/exposure and relevant yield/duration/holdings risks. Fund AUM is not corporate market capitalization.
- **ARM, MU, MRVL, BE, GEV, NBIS, LITE, TEAM, BABA, JD, NVO:** source/accounting/cycle/currency/current-earning-capacity limits make a smooth EPS target unsuitable. Each has a substantive Valuation section and actual historical arithmetic where available; omitted target does not mean omitted research. Read the specific reasons rather than treating all omissions as negative earnings.

## Material evidence limitations

Every final saved audit is **PROVISIONAL**, with no error-severity finding in the final native runs. This is not PASS or independent source-truth certification. Typical gaps are a single matched-date quote/share/capitalization publisher, exact current shares, estimated debt-component aggregation and modeled/translated fundamentals. Issuer releases, annual filings and hosting mirrors are one originating publisher; successful transcription does not prove the underlying economics.

International securities retain ADR/ADS ratios and conversion dates/rates. USD convenience translations and separately estimated conversions are not interchangeable with contemporaneous USD earning capacity or future FX forecasts. PDD's audited FY2025 differs from the preliminary March release; the audited authority and superseded figures are preserved. TCEHY's price-times-shares discrepancy remains **2.2305%**, beyond the native 1% comparison threshold and explicitly warned. JD's comparable current market capitalization remains unavailable after conflicting observations; no capitalization or FCF-yield certainty was invented. BABA/PDD vendor snapshot estimates disclose conflicts with historical rows instead of falsely counting them as observations of the same dated fact. [Alternative observed ADR statistics and limitations](adr-market-observations.json) remain available.

Fund disclosures retain their distinct dates. QQQ sponsor holdings-count definitions conflict and an anomalous historical PDF price was excluded from canonical pricing. SOXX's announced later split was not applied early. TLT's SEC yield, trailing distributions, YTM, duration and sponsor convexity convention are not interchangeable with a guaranteed price return.
