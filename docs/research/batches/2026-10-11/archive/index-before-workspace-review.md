# Research index

[Run guidance](README.md)

Saved audits describe historical offline structure/arithmetic, not source truth or investment approval; coverage counts nonempty graph sections, not verification. Conditional model targets are earnings-multiple USD/share outputs, not DCFs, fair values or guaranteed outcomes; display rounding does not change full precision, source periods or timestamps retained in the data and reports.

Targets are probability-weighted discounted present values, not future trading prices. The model horizon is an assumption in years, used for EPS growth, discounting and conditional price CAGR. Model EPS anchor periods and reported/estimated/assumed kinds are shown below: alignment with a future price exit requires an explicit convention. Price CAGR is not EPS growth or observed YoY performance; missing alignment keeps audits PROVISIONAL.
