# Decision

**Inputs:** Valuation plus the completed prerequisite sections and outstanding risks.

**Steps:**
1. Summarize business quality (Duan), durability (Buffett), stewardship (Duan/Buffett), inversion risks (Munger), structural opportunity (Li Lu) and price separately. For each give evidence, confidence and a potential disconfirming observation.
2. State a research conclusion: attractive for further diligence, watchlist, or avoid; distinguish business attractiveness from price attractiveness. When provisional, no validated target or definitive allocation recommendation.
3. Give thesis-breaking conditions, observable review triggers and unresolved primary-research questions. For sparse coverage, suggest product/customer/supply-chain diligence rather than inventing answers.
4. Separate AI research confidence (coverage and source quality) from investment certainty (economic resilience and range of outcomes). Explain consensus dependence and unknown unknowns.

**Output:** `## Decision`, concise evidence-linked decision memo and review criteria; no simulated investor quotes, orders or brokerage instructions.

**Failure path:** material evidence/model gaps => explicitly conditional research conclusion. A model result cannot override unresolved integrity or circle-of-competence concerns.

**Question:** If trading stopped for five years, which evidence—not the spreadsheet—would support continued ownership?
