# Inversion

**Inputs:** Moat and Management conclusions, including their counterevidence and gaps.

**Steps:**
1. Build failure paths from the specific advantage and allocation claims: competitive displacement, bad acquisitions, governance failure, regulation, supply disruption, concentration and financing stress.
2. Seek the strongest informed bear case, not a weak strawman. Compare historical analogues, explaining differences; use competition, incentives and technology-adoption lenses without pretending analogy proves an outcome.
3. For each risk identify mechanism, timing, severity, early warning, mitigation and valuation treatment. Give probabilities only as explicit justified assumptions; avoid spurious precision.
4. Check anchoring, narrative, confirmation and survivorship bias. Separate ordinary operating variability from discrete catastrophic events; disclose tail events not captured by the three scenarios.

**Output:** `## Inversion`, risk register and falsification criteria feeding Valuation; document unmodeled risks.

**Failure path:** an unquantifiable material risk stays explicit and can justify research avoidance; do not conceal it inside a higher discount rate or assign an invented probability.

**Question:** Where am I most likely wrong, and why would a knowledgeable investor refuse to own this business?
