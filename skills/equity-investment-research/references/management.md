# Management

**Inputs:** Business economics and grounded filings, compensation/proxy disclosures and capital-allocation history.

**Steps:**
1. Review dated major decisions, original rationale, subsequent results and alternatives; avoid scoring only by stock performance. Cover R&D, acquisitions, dividends, repurchases and dilution.
2. Assess candor by comparing commitments with outcomes, treatment of failures and adjusted-metric disclosures. Distinguish economic ownership from voting control and dual-class rights.
3. Review incentives, compensation, related-party transactions, succession, executive turnover and organization dependence on one person. Link capital allocation to business reinvestment needs.

**Output:** `## Management`, decision/result table, alignment and integrity assessment, succession risks and unknowns; apply Duan's “right people” and Buffett's stewardship lenses.

**Failure path:** missing proxy or decision evidence => unverified alignment claims; serious unresolved integrity issues are thesis veto candidates, not a discount-rate adjustment.

**Question:** Would this business retain its strengths if its CEO retired, and does management treat outside owners fairly?
