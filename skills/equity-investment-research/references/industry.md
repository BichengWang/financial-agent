# Industry

**Inputs:** Grounding sources, sector evidence and dated competitor/customer/supplier observations.

**Steps:**
1. Apply Li Lu's civilization-evolution lens: identify enduring demand, productivity gains and value-chain position versus fashionable narratives.
2. Distinguish sourced market estimates from company-promoted TAM; identify serviceable market, adoption constraints, saturation and double counting.
3. Map competitor economics, substitutes, customer/supplier bargaining power, regulation and alternative technology routes. Historical technological revolutions are hypotheses, not forecasts.
4. Translate structural trends into bounded scenario drivers and limitations for Valuation. State which evidence would show the industry tailwind does not accrue to this company.

**Output:** `## Industry`, value-chain map in prose/table, structural drivers, constraints and sourced/assumed growth boundaries.

**Failure path:** unavailable market-size evidence => omit numerical TAM; uncertain route or market capture => wider conditional scenarios and provisional claims.

**Question:** In twenty years, would this firm be an enduring institution or a transient beneficiary of this cycle?
